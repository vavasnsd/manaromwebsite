    HR Psychotest

    Psychological testing for HR

    Helps organisations choose the right people. Results are interpreted by our psychiatrists and psychologists, with advice throughout.

    BCABasic Competency Assessment

    Screens applicants on the spot. Works for every position and education level.

    MCAManarom Cognitive Ability Assessment

    Measures intelligence and aptitude, the test most closely linked to success at work.

    MPIManarom Personality Inventory

    Assesses work personality, creativity, leadership and openness to learning.

    MRTManarom Resilience Psychotest

    Assesses how well people manage emotions and pressure when the unexpected happens.
